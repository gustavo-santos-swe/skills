# AI tells — short list

Scan for these first. Full catalog: [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (curl once per session if needed). Structural filler also in stop-slop `phrases` / `structures`.

## Chatbot / openers / closers

- Great question / Absolutely / Sure! / Let me… / Looking at your…
- I hope this helps / Let me know if you need anything / Happy to dig deeper
- Here’s the thing / Here’s what / To answer your question

## Punctuation & layout

- Em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) used as AI breaks — **ban** (period, comma, colon, parens)
- `- **Label:**` vertical bold lists
- Title Case Headings that aren’t proper nouns
- Decorative emoji in durable prose

## Vocabulary (high-frequency)

testament, pivotal, landscape (abstract), delve, tapestry, underscore, vibrant, foster, showcase, crucial, intricate, align with, additionally (as glue)

## Significance inflation

- “marks a pivotal moment”, “broader trends”, “stands as a…”
- Fake depth with trailing `-ing`: highlighting…, ensuring…, showcasing…, fostering…

## Formula structures

- It’s not X, it’s Y / Not only… but… / Not A. Not B. C.
- Rule-of-three padding
- Generic conclusions: only time will tell, the future is bright

## Hedging / filler

worth noting, interestingly, surprisingly, in today’s…, at its core, when it comes to, really / simply / actually (empty)

## False agency

the decision emerges, the data tells us, the market rewards — name the actor

## Repo-specific

Diff-anchored prose: “this function was added to replace…” → say what it *does* and *why*, not the git story
